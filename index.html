How long does a repair take?

Most repairs are completed in 30–60 minutes. Many iPhone screen repairs take approximately 15–20 minutes.

Do you come to my location?

Yes. We come to your home, office, workplace, or another convenient location in our service area.

Do you offer warranty?

Yes. PhoneFix2You repairs include a 30-day warranty for added peace of mind.

What devices do you repair?

We repair Apple iPhone, Samsung Galaxy, Google Pixel, Motorola, and many other smartphones.

How do I book a repair?

Use the Book Repair button, call us, or send a text. Choose your device, repair, preferred time, and service address.

Do you accept same-day appointments?

Yes, same-day appointments are available when scheduling allows. Contact us early for the best availability.
